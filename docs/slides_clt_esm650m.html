The Project

What I am building in the SAGE Lab

  • Building a cross-layer transcoder (CLT) for ESM2-650M to perform unsupervised zero-shot mutation effect prediction.
  • Extending mechanistic interpretability techniques — used on language models — to protein language models at scale.
  • Constructing biological circuits from sparse CLT features to test whether ESM2-650M encodes structurally and evolutionarily meaningful representations when reasoning about mutations.
  • Codebase is a fork of EleutherAI's sparsify, extended with CLT support, tensor parallelism, bfloat16 training, and KL+FVU loss.
Motivation

Why mechanistic interpretability for proteins?

  • ESM-2 predicts structure and function from sequence and underpins much of modern computational biology — but how it computes contacts, folds, and functional sites is opaque.
  • Extracting causal circuits yields scientific hypotheses about protein biology, not just predictions — e.g. "a catalytic-motif feature at layer 6 gates a domain-recognition feature at layer 20."
  • Two recent results define the frontier: Nainani et al. 2025 (SAE latents causally mediate contact prediction) and ProtoMech 2026 (CLTs recover 82–89% of performance at ESM2-8M/35M).
  • The gap: nobody has trained CLTs at 650M — rich enough to matter biologically, but the training cost explodes. That is this project.
Method

SAE to Transcoder to Cross-Layer Transcoder

                 reads          predicts (reconstructs)
  SAE            act at L    ->   same act at L
  Transcoder     input of L  ->   OUTPUT of L
  CLT (mine)     input of L  ->   outputs of L, L+1, ..., L+N
  • Real biological computation flows across layers. A CLT encoder fires once at layer L and its decoder emits into every downstream target — so the learned dictionary is the cross-layer wiring.
  • TopK activation (Gao 2024) fires exactly k latents per token, no L1 tuning. Expansion 128 x d_model 1280 gives 163,840 latents per layer.
Progress

Progress till now — what runs today

  • ESM-2 650M integrated into the sparsify pipeline: CLS-token handling, no-BOS, hookpoints before layernorm.
  • Cross-layer transcoder training working with configurable reach and four coalescing strategies (none, concat, per-layer, group).
  • KL + FVU training implemented — reconstruct well and stay faithful to ESM-2's computation.
  • Tensor parallelism via DTensor plus bfloat16 for weights that exceed a single GPU.
  • Sweep analysis ranking W&B runs by FVU, replacement-CE increase, L0, and dead-latent percentage.
  • Current focus (Objective 1): making 650M CLT training fast and memory-tractable so the recipe can scale.
The Hard Problem

Why is 650M CLT training slow?

BottleneckWhat happens
1. No activation cachingFull frozen ESM-2 650M forward pass every step (two passes for KL-FVU); every sweep re-runs ESM-2 from scratch.
2. Enormous CLT sizeAt EF=128, cross_layer=12: about 89B params across 33 layers — larger than ESM-2 itself, ~121x the 35M recipe.
3. Scale and commsTopK over 163,840 latents x 32K tokens; DTensor all-gather each layer; custom backward blocks torch.compile.
Future — Efficiency

Efficiency roadmap (impact to effort)

  • Profile first — torch.profiler / nsys to split time between ESM-2 forward and CLT before changing anything.
  • Activation caching — precompute and memory-map ESM-2 activations; expected 3–10x speedup, sweeps become near-free.
  • Shrink the CLT — start EF=32, cross_layer=4–6, layer_stride=2; reproduce ProtoMech-quality at reduced scale first.
  • LoRA-style factorization — shared base dictionary plus small per-target low-rank decoder adapters; low-rank encoder.
  • Loop-level wins — torch.compile coverage, 8-bit Adam, activation checkpointing, TP comms overlap.

Future — The Science

Then: go after the biology

  • Reproduce and sharpen Nainani's motif to domain contact circuit with CLTs (which model actual cross-layer wiring) via activation patching.
  • Circuit discovery at 650M — ProtoMech-style greedy latent selection plus integrated gradients. Do larger models use the same circuits at higher resolution?
  • Causal validation — ProteinGym: test whether circuit-relevant CLT features alone predict mutation effects.
  • Scale out — carry the optimized pipeline to ESM-C 3B–6B, the target that makes the efficiency work worth doing now.
